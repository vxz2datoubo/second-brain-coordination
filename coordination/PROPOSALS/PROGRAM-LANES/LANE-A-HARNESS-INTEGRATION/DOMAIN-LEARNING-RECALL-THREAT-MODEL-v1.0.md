# Domain Learning Recall Threat Model v1.0

R140 fails closed against the following false-green classes:

1. **Nearest-text authority substitution**: a semantically similar case is treated as applicable despite model/version, scene or explicit constraint mismatch.
2. **Maturity laundering**: a candidate/conflicted/needs-revalidation object is presented as stable guidance.
3. **Counterexample blindness**: failure conditions or counterexamples are read but not reflected in ranking/abstention.
4. **Domain-truth duplication**: Second Brain copies a final lesson body and later drifts from AI Film canonical truth.
5. **Stale-revision recall**: a bundle is emitted after the exact domain revision changes without re-resolution.
6. **Process/outcome conflation**: successful reads/tests are reported as proof that the recalled advice is creatively correct.
7. **Confounded A/B laundering**: `CD25-KAIM-WINDOW-AB-20260815` becomes a clean SOAC superiority claim.
8. **One-shot universalization**: an excellent case becomes a universal rule or Formal Skill without domain-owned promotion evidence.
9. **Private-data leakage**: raw private conversation/media/secrets enter public fixtures, receipts or logs.
10. **Hidden mutation**: a read-only smoke modifies AI Film checkout, opens a domain PR, or creates a generic cross-repo writer.

Required controls: exact revision binding; read-set/authority-path proofs; multi-axis applicability gates; explicit abstention; failure/counterexample checks; model/version compatibility; public-safe opaque refs; clean-before/after domain status; append-only evidence receipts; negative replay; Python 3.11/3.13 exact-head CI; retained R136-R139 regressions.

Stop conditions: any required AI Film/domain write, private/secret/permission/production action, authority-source replacement, destructive history operation, real trading/funds action, or write surface beyond a future exact R140 allowlist.
