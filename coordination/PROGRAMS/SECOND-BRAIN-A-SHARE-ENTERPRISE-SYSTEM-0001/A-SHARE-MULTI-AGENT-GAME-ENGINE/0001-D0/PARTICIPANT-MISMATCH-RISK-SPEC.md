# Participant Mismatch Risk Specification

Status: `Interface / Candidate Only`.

Mismatch risk records how much a proposed action or narrative depends on a participant-family hypothesis that could be wrong. Inputs are posterior dispersion, alternative hypotheses, evidence coverage, capability gaps, rule-snapshot availability, time-horizon disagreement and counterevidence. It should increase when multiple families explain the same trace or when the action is infeasible under T+1, price-limit, suspension or inventory constraints.

It is not a probability of wrongdoing, manipulation or a real actor. It must block confidence escalation when critical evidence is missing and has no authority to create a trade recommendation.
