# Level-k and Self-play Plan

Status: `Future Roadmap`; `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`.

Level-k means a registered hierarchy of candidate beliefs about candidate policies. It does not mean that a market actor literally reasons at level *k*. Self-play measures synthetic interaction robustness, not evidence of A-share participant intent.

## Promotion gates

1. D1 deterministic rules MVP passes its entire fixture and invariant suite.
2. D2 data admission, if empirical calibration is requested, proves permission, semantics, point-in-time availability and rule/status versions.
3. D3 demonstrates predeclared baseline comparison, calibration, coverage and out-of-sample safeguards.
4. D4 fixes the policy set, hierarchy depth, priors, action space, scenario count, seed count, compute cap and cancellation threshold before any run.
5. D5 supplies reproducible seed results, exploitability metrics with units, failure cases and independent review.

## Rejection conditions

Reject or defer if the action space requires unavailable raw events; a rule snapshot is UNKNOWN; a new level only improves in-sample fit; seed/regime results are unstable; variant count expands after results; compute ceiling is exceeded; or the claimed increment over the rules baseline is not demonstrated. MARL requires a new user-gated task after D5 and has no authorization in D0.
