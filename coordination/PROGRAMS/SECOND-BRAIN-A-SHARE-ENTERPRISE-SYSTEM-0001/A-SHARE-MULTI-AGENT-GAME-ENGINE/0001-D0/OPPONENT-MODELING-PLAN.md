# Opponent Modeling Plan

Status: `Interface / Future Roadmap`.

Start with a finite set of family and subtype hypotheses. Maintain a posterior distribution, retain alternatives and permit abstention. Update only through governed evidence likelihoods and source-dependence controls; do not derive posteriors from prose fluency or repeated correlated narratives.

The initial rules-based model may output candidate responses for synthetic states. Bayesian updates require a pre-registered prior, likelihood source, missing-data treatment, calibration rule and failure condition. Any empirical update needs point-in-time labels or independently auditable proxy outcomes. Outputs stay within research hypotheses and cannot emit trades.
