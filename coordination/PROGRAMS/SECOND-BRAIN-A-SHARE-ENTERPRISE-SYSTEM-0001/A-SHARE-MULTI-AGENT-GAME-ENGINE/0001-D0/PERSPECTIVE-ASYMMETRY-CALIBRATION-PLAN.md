# Perspective Asymmetry Calibration Plan

Calibration is claim-class specific. Self-description, observations about others, institutional-rule claims, market-structure claims and directional forecasts receive separate resolution ledgers. A correct institutional fact does not validate a directional forecast, and a high-profile source does not provide independent corroboration for its own restatement.

The first executable validation task must use public-safe, pre-registered claims with explicit timestamps and resolution windows. It must compare claim-class calibration against a baseline, preserve misses, and audit correlation among sources. Until that task exists, every profile's calibration history is `UNKNOWN`.
