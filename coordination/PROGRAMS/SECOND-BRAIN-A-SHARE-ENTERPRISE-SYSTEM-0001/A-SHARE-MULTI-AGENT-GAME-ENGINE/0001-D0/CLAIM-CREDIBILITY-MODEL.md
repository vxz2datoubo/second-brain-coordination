# Claim Credibility Model

`ClaimCredibilityVector` is a review aid, not a truth score. It records provenance, claim specificity, testability, independence, timeliness, conflict risk and resolved-calibration history separately. No weighted sum may be treated as a factual identity probability without a separately approved and calibrated model.

For every claim, the reviewer must list what the claim supports, what it does not support, direct incentives, shared-source dependence, official evidence needed for institutional facts, counterevidence, a resolution rule and a forbidden downstream use. A source can be more credible about a narrow operational observation than its market-direction forecast; the dimensions must not be collapsed merely because the speaker is well known.
