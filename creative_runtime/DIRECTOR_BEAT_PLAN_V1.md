# DirectorBeatPlan/v1

`DirectorBeatPlan/v1` is a deterministic, immutable, read-only directing plan
for one explicit authored choice and scene. It joins an inspected
`DirectorBrief/v2`, `StaticStoryGraph/v1`, and `StoryBibleBundle/v1` from the
same exact `ScriptPackage/v1` identity.

The plan binds the package, brief, graph, bible, style, choice, scene, character
anchors and scene anchor. Its ordered beats cover setup, dramatic question,
character knowledge continuity, scene staging, equal option presentation,
static consequence previews and continuity-safe exits. Four approved styles may
change presentation intent only; they cannot change legal options or story
consequences.

Choice, scene and option arguments are content addresses. Compiling or
inspecting one does not mean a player selected it and creates no campaign,
session, progress cursor, ledger event, director job, media job or provider
request. Inspection recompiles from source truth, so replacing content and
recomputing an outer hash still fails closed.
