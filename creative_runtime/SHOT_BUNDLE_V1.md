# ShotBundle/v1

`ShotBundle/v1` is an immutable provider-neutral production blueprint compiled
from a freshly inspected `DirectorBeatPlan/v1`. Each ordered
`ShotResponsibility/v1` declares dramatic coverage, framing, subjects, spatial
and staging anchors, symbolic movement, bounded duration, continuity, audio and
asset roles. Every director beat and every legal option must remain covered.

The bundle is not a render request, provider payload, queue item or execution
record. It cannot enqueue, generate, upload, retry, persist player progress or
mutate story state. Inspection recompiles from source truth, so recomputed outer
hashes cannot legitimize forged inner shot content.
